KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS
Healthcare & Pharmaceutical Business Analysis


1. Pediatrics has the highest total charges

Finding:
Pediatrics generated the highest total charges in the dataset.

Meaning:
Pediatrics is an important contributor to overall healthcare charges.

Recommendation:
Maintain sufficient staff and resources in Pediatrics to support patient demand.


2. Pediatrics has the highest waiting time

Finding:
Pediatrics has the highest average waiting time at 35 minutes.

Meaning:
The department has both high charges and high waiting time, which may indicate higher operational workload.

Recommendation:
Review staffing and appointment scheduling in Pediatrics to reduce waiting time.


3. Appointment cancellation rate is 5%

Finding:
5% of appointments were cancelled.

Meaning:
Most appointments were completed, but some appointments did not result in completed visits.

Recommendation:
Monitor cancellations and use appointment reminders to reduce avoidable cancellations.


4. Follow-up visits generate the highest charges

Finding:
Follow-up visits generate the highest total charges among visit types.

Meaning:
Follow-up services are an important contributor to charges and patient activity.

Recommendation:
Ensure sufficient appointment capacity for follow-up services.


5. Dr. Meera has the highest workload

Finding:
Dr. Meera has the highest number of appointments.

Meaning:
Dr. Meera has the highest patient demand/workload among the doctors.

Recommendation:
Monitor workload and consider better appointment distribution if demand remains high.
